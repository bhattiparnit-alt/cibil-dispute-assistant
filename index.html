Master Your Credit Health

  • Pay on Time: Never miss a credit card or loan EMI due date.
  • Keep Utilization Low: Try to use less than 30% of your total credit limit.
  • Don't Apply Excessively: Avoid applying for multiple loans/credit cards at once.
  • Check Your Report: Review your CIBIL report regularly for errors.
  • Maintain Old Accounts: A longer credit history is better for your score.

About CIBIL App
